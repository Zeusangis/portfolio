ClimateCrop AI

climate-driven planting decisions

Turns public climate and crop records into planting decisions for one farm. A farmer saves their farm once; the app ranks which crops fit, finds when to plant, compares seasons, and follows a crop from sowing to harvest with an expert alongside. Every number comes from a named source, and AI only writes advice text from facts the app hands it. Built with a teammate for TechXpo 2026 at the University of Louisiana Monroe.

  • Crop fit is scored by stated rules (60% weakest factor + 40% average of warmth, water, soil and pH) against a 30-year NASA POWER climate normal
  • Crop expert output must match a fixed format and is rejected if it gives a chemical dose or a number not in the facts, falling back to general guidance
  • Field alerts for frost, heat, heavy rain and dry spells from the real forecast
  • Leaf-photo disease checks with Pl@ntNet, with Gemini as a second look when unsure
  • Cookie auth through a same-origin proxy, CI on every push, Render + Vercel deploys

Dendrite

local-first knowledge graph

A knowledge graph for Markdown notes that runs entirely on your machine. The Markdown vault stays the source of truth; Dendrite builds a searchable, explainable graph of notes, links and related activity on top of it.

  • Go API with SQLite persistence and background file sync for edits made outside the app
  • Atomic same-directory writes, strict vault paths and symlink rejection
  • Revision checks reject stale saves and deletes with HTTP 409
  • Full-text search, wikilinks, aliases, backlinks and undo/redo
  • Optional macOS activity collector and browser extension, with no keystrokes or page contents recorded

TrueHire

evidence-first technical recruiting

A recruiting platform that pairs job-specific resume analysis with bounded evidence from a candidate's public GitHub. AI supplies evidence for a human decision; it never accepts or rejects anyone on its own.

  • Celery and Redis run screening in the background after each application
  • Resume and GitHub analysis are independent, durable, and retry with backoff
  • Recruiter auth, CSRF protection and organization-scoped data access
  • Append-only audit trail of every stage change and analysis retry
  • Alembic migrations, automated tests, linting and CI

AthleteX

training load and ACL injury risk

Training load and ACL injury risk for coaches and athletes on one screen. Athletes log a short daily check-in; coaches see the whole squad, spot who needs attention, and score landing mechanics from a jump video.

  • Pose detection measures knee flexion, knee valgus and trunk lean from video
  • Risk is scored low (0–1), moderate (2–3) or high (4+)
  • Role-based access: coaches manage athletes, athletes see only their own data
  • Training assistant answers questions using each athlete's profile and logs
